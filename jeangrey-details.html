Jean Grey

Jean Grey is a member of a subspecies of humans known as mutants, individuals born with superhuman abilities with Jean possessing psionic powers. Initially capable of using only telekinesis, she later develops the powers of telepathy and empathy. Jean is a caring and nurturing figure, but struggles with the pressure of being both an Omega-level mutant and the physical manifestation of the Phoenix Force, an extremely powerful cosmic entity.
