Iceman Details

Bobby Drake is a mutant with the capacity of generating extreme cold and convert his own body into ice, acting as the charismatic super hero known as the astonishing Iceman.[19] When his powers first manifested, Drake was hunted by his intolerant neighbors, ultimately finding sanctuary in Professor Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters. As Iceman, he became the youngest member of the original X-Men.
